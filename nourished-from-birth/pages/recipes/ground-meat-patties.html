Ground Meat Patties

Prep10 min
Cook15–20 min
ServesFamily + baby
Age9 months+
Storage2 days fridge / 3 months freezer

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef, lamb, or bison (grass-fed, organic or pasture-raised)
  • 1 medium onion, grated (squeeze out excess liquid)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Ghee or butter for cooking
  • Salt to taste (omit under 12 months)
Optional
  • ½ tsp sumac
Some grocery stores and butchers carry ground beef, lamb, or bison with organ meats already blended in — look for labels that say "ancestral blend." This is the most nutritious option and requires no extra preparation.

Instructions

Option A — Pan-fry

  1. 1Grate the onion and squeeze out as much liquid as possible using your hands or a clean cloth. This keeps the patties from falling apart.
  2. 2Combine ground meat, grated onion, egg, turmeric, coriander, pepper, and sumac (if using) in a bowl. Mix well until fully combined.
  3. 3Form into small patties about 2 inches wide and ½ inch thick.
  4. 4Melt ghee in a pan over medium heat. Cook patties for 3–4 minutes per side until cooked through with no pink remaining. Do not press with a spatula — this forces out the juices and dries the patty out.
  5. 5Let cool slightly before serving.

Option B — Bake

  1. 1Grate the onion and squeeze out as much liquid as possible using your hands or a clean cloth.
  2. 2Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
  3. 3Combine ground meat, grated onion, egg, turmeric, coriander, pepper, and sumac (if using) in a bowl. Mix well until fully combined.
  4. 4Form into small patties about 2 inches wide and ½ inch thick. Place on a lined baking tray.
  5. 5Bake for 18–20 minutes until cooked through with no pink remaining.
  6. 6Let cool slightly before serving.

How to Serve by Age

AgeHow to serveAmount
9–12 monthsWhole patty as finger food — soft enough to squish between gums1 patty
12–18 monthsWhole patty or cut in half, with a dipping sauce1–2 patties
18 months+Family portions, seasoned with salt to tasteAs desired

⚠️ Always cook ground meat to an internal temperature of 160°F (71°C) — no pink remaining. The patty should feel soft when pressed gently between two fingers before serving to baby.

Storage

🧊 Fridge

Up to 2 days in a sealed container.

❄️ Freezer

Up to 3 months. Layer raw or cooked patties between parchment paper and freeze. Reheat gently on the stovetop or in the oven.

Making it Fun

🍟 Make it a family meal — serve with homemade french fries, steamed or baked vegetables, rice, or bulgur. Baby gets their patty, the whole family eats the same meal.
🥑 Add a dipping sauce — plain yogurt, mashed avocado, or unsweetened applesauce makes it easier for younger babies to swallow and adds extra nutrition.
🍋 Finish with lemon — for family portions, a squeeze of lemon and an extra pinch of sumac before serving is the traditional way to finish these.

Why This Nourishes

🩸
Iron
Ground beef, lamb, and bison are among the richest sources of heme iron — the most absorbable form for growing babies
💪
Zinc
Supports immune function, healthy growth, and brain development — abundant in all three meat options
⚡
Vitamin B12
Essential for nervous system development and healthy red blood cells
🌿
Turmeric
A traditional anti-inflammatory spice used across Persian and Middle Eastern cooking for centuries
🧠
Healthy Fats
Grass-fed meat and ghee provide fat-soluble vitamins and omega-3s critical for brain development
