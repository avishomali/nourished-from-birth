Creamy Coconut Amaranth Porridge

This creamy amaranth porridge has become a favorite with my kids. The amaranth is slowly simmered with coconut milk, cinnamon, and vanilla until thick and tender. It makes a warm, filling breakfast and is easy to customize with fruit, nut butter, or other toppings your family enjoys.

CookAbout 20 min
Rest5 min
Age6 months+

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry amaranth, rinsed well in a fine-mesh strainer
  • 1 cup full-fat canned coconut milk
  • 1½ cups filtered water, plus a little more if needed
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (omit for babies under 12 months)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons pure maple syrup, or to taste (optional for little ones)
Optional toppings
  • Sliced or finely chopped pear
  • Berries
  • Banana
  • Toasted pecans
  • Shredded coconut
  • Nut or seed butter
  • Full-fat yogurt
  • Extra coconut milk

Instructions

  1. 1 Add the rinsed amaranth, coconut milk, water, cinnamon, and salt to a medium saucepan.
  2. 2 Bring to a gentle boil over medium-high heat.
  3. 3 As soon as it begins to boil, reduce the heat to low and cover with a tight-fitting lid.
  4. 4 Simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring once or twice during cooking to prevent the grains from sticking to the bottom.
  5. 5 Once the liquid has been absorbed and the amaranth is tender, remove the saucepan from the heat.
  6. 6 Stir in the vanilla and maple syrup.
  7. 7 Cover again and let the porridge rest for about 5 minutes. It will continue to thicken as it sits.
  8. 8 Spoon into bowls. If the porridge becomes thicker than you like, stir in a splash of warm water, coconut milk, or regular milk before serving.
  9. 9 Add your favorite toppings and serve warm.
For babies and toddlers: For younger children, you can leave out the maple syrup and serve the porridge with naturally sweet fruit such as mashed banana, pear, or berries. Thin the porridge with a little extra milk or water if needed to make the texture appropriate for your child. Nut or seed butter can also be stirred in for extra creaminess if the allergen has already been introduced.

Adaptations by Age

AgeHow to serve
6 to 12 months Leave out the maple syrup and the salt. Thin the porridge with warm water, breast milk, formula, or milk if your baby is already having it, until smooth. Top with mashed banana, pear, or berries.
Toddlers Serve with finely chopped fruit, ground nuts or a spoonful of nut or seed butter, and a little full-fat yogurt.
Family Add maple syrup to taste and top with toasted pecans, shredded coconut, berries, or extra coconut milk.

Notes

  • Texture: Amaranth has a naturally soft, slightly sticky texture once cooked, so this porridge will be thicker and creamier than oatmeal.
  • Thick coconut milk: If your coconut milk is especially thick, you may need a little extra water toward the end of cooking.

Storage

🧊 Fridge

Up to 2 days in an airtight container. The porridge will thicken considerably as it cools, so add a splash of water or milk when reheating and stir until creamy again.
