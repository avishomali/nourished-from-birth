Butternut Squash Egg Crepes

Soft, lightly sweet crepes made with egg, butternut squash, whole milk, and butter. They're easy to make, naturally soft, and work well for babies, toddlers, and the whole family.

Total timeAbout 15 min
Makes6 to 8 small crepes
Age6 months+

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup smooth cooked butternut squash purée
  • ½ cup flour, we use Molino Grassi Italian 00 flour
  • ½ cup whole milk
  • 2 tsp melted grass-fed butter
  • Small pinch of cinnamon, optional
  • Butter or ghee, for cooking

Why these work so well This is a simple way to work a vegetable into breakfast without anyone noticing. The butternut squash gives the crepes a naturally sweet flavor and a soft, tender texture, and the whole batch comes together in about 15 minutes. They are soft enough to serve plain to a baby or dressed up with fruit and yogurt for the rest of the family.

Instructions

  1. 1 Add the eggs, butternut squash purée, flour, milk, melted butter, and optional cinnamon to a bowl.
  2. 2 Whisk until smooth and well combined.
  3. 3 Let the batter sit for about 5 minutes while you warm the pan.
  4. 4 Heat a small skillet over medium-low heat and add a little butter or ghee. We use our Our Place pan for this.
  5. 5 Pour a small amount of batter into the pan and quickly tilt the skillet so the batter spreads into a thin layer.
  6. 6 Cook for about 1 to 2 minutes, until the edges begin to set and the bottom is lightly golden.
  7. 7 Carefully flip and cook for another 30 to 60 seconds on the second side.
  8. 8 Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining batter, adding more butter or ghee as needed.
Batter consistency: The batter should be thin enough to spread easily when you tilt the pan. If your butternut squash purée is very thick, add 1 to 2 tablespoons extra whole milk until the batter reaches a thin crepe consistency.
For baby: Let the crepes cool, then cut or tear them into a size and shape appropriate for your baby's eating skills. They are soft enough to serve plain, or with a thin spread of tahini, nut butter, full-fat yogurt, mashed fruit, or butter.

Serving Ideas

  • Tahini
  • Nut butter
  • Full-fat yogurt
  • Mashed fruit
  • Butter
  • For a bigger breakfast, serve alongside egg, fruit, or yogurt

Adaptations by Age

AgeHow to serve
6 months+ Let cool, then cut or tear into a size and shape appropriate for your baby's eating skills. Serve plain or with a thin spread of full-fat yogurt or mashed fruit.
Toddlers Cut into strips or small pieces for self-feeding, with a thin spread of tahini or nut butter if desired.
Family Serve with egg, fruit, yogurt, tahini, or nut butter for a complete breakfast.

Storage

Make-Ahead Friendly

These can be made ahead and reheated gently in a skillet or toaster oven.

Recipe Notes

  • Butternut squash: Use a very smooth purée so the batter stays even and the crepes cook uniformly.
  • Pan temperature: Medium-low works best. If the pan is too hot, the outside can brown before the crepe has time to set.
