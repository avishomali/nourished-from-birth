Warm-Spiced Beef & Bean Chili

A hearty beef chili made with soaked beans, tomato sauce, and warm spices like turmeric, coriander, cardamom, cinnamon, paprika, and fennel.

MethodLong simmer
Time3 to 4 hours
PlusOvernight soak
Age9 months+

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 cup dried beans, preferably a mix of pinto and Great Northern beans, soaked overnight
  • 2 cans tomato sauce, 8 oz each
  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp turmeric, divided
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ⅛ tsp ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
  • ⅛ tsp cayenne, optional
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¾ to 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • Butter, ghee, or another cooking fat, for browning

Instructions

  1. 1 Cover the dried pinto and Great Northern beans generously with filtered water and soak overnight (why we soak legumes). The next day, drain and rinse well.
  2. 2 Heat a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat and add a little butter, ghee, or cooking fat.
  3. 3 Add the onion and cook until soft and translucent.
  4. 4 Add ½ teaspoon of the turmeric to the onion and stir for about 30 seconds.
  5. 5 Increase the heat to medium-high and add the ground beef.
  6. 6 Brown the beef well, breaking it apart as it cooks. Try not to stir constantly. We want the beef to actually brown rather than steam in its own juices.
  7. 7 Once the beef is browned, add the remaining ½ teaspoon turmeric, cinnamon, coriander, paprika, cardamom, fennel seeds, cayenne if using, black pepper, and salt. Stir for about 30 seconds.
  8. 8 Add the soaked and drained beans, tomato sauce, filtered water, and bay leaves.
  9. 9 Stir well and bring to a gentle boil.
  10. 10 Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer gently for 3 to 4 hours, stirring occasionally.
  11. 11 Check the chili from time to time. If it becomes too thick before the beans are tender, add a little more hot filtered water.
  12. 12 Once the beans are fully tender and the chili has thickened, remove the bay leaves.
  13. 13 Taste and adjust the salt or spices if needed.

Bean Notes

Why pinto and Great Northern For this long-cooked version, I use pinto and Great Northern beans. They hold up better to a long simmer and give the chili a nice mix of creamy and firm texture.

If You Want Kidney Beans

Use canned kidney beans instead of dried. Drain and rinse them, then add them during the last 30 to 60 minutes of cooking so they don't fall apart.
For little ones: Keep the cayenne very light or leave it out entirely. You can mash some of the beans into the chili and break the beef into smaller pieces depending on your child's eating skills.

Serving Ideas

  • Avocado
  • Full-fat yogurt or sour cream
  • Shredded cheese
  • Rice
  • Sourdough bread
  • Extra butter or ghee, if you like

Adaptations by Age

AgeHow to serve
9 to 12 months Leave out the cayenne, mash the beans well, and break the beef into very small pieces. Serve a spoonful with yogurt or avocado, and set aside their portion before adding extra salt.
Toddlers Mash some of the beans for a thicker texture and serve with rice or sourdough bread for dipping.
Family Add the cayenne if you like heat, and top with avocado, sour cream, or shredded cheese.

Storage

🧊 Fridge

Up to 2 days in an airtight container. Why we keep cooked meat for 2 days only. Reheat gently, adding a splash of filtered water if the chili has thickened.
