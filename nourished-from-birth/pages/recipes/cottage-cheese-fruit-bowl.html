Cottage Cheese & Fruit Bowl

Creamy full-fat cottage cheese paired with soft, naturally sweet fruit. An incredibly easy protein-rich snack that takes just a few minutes to prepare for babies, toddlers, and the whole family.

Prep5 min
CookNone
Serves1 to 2
Age6 months+

Ingredients

  • ½ cup full-fat cottage cheese — we use Good Culture or Nancy's
  • ½ cup soft fruit, chopped or mashed: banana, strawberries, blueberries, ripe peach, or ripe pear
  • Small pinch of ground cinnamon, optional
Optional add-ins
  • 1 tbsp finely ground walnuts (we use Diamond brand from Target) — finely ground rather than pieces for babies and younger children
  • 1 tbsp finely ground pumpkin seeds — we get ours from Costco
  • 1 tbsp smooth nut or seed butter
  • Raw honey, to taste — 12 months+ only. We use Lineage Provisions or Honey Pacifica

Why this works so well This is the kind of snack that solves a real problem: something quick, genuinely nourishing, and requiring zero cooking. Full-fat cottage cheese brings a meaningful amount of protein and fat, which is exactly what keeps babies and toddlers satisfied between meals. Pair it with any soft, ripe fruit you have on hand and you have a complete snack in under five minutes.

Instructions

  1. 1 Add the cottage cheese. Place the cottage cheese in a small bowl. For babies or younger toddlers who are not yet comfortable with larger curds, mash it gently with a fork to make it smoother.
  2. 2 Prepare the fruit. Use ripe, soft fruit and prepare it according to your child's eating ability. Mash banana with a fork. Finely chop or mash strawberries. Gently crush or mash blueberries, especially for younger children. Finely chop or mash a very ripe peach. Use a ripe, soft pear, or lightly steam a firm one until soft. Add the prepared fruit to the cottage cheese.
  3. 3 Add your extras. Stir in a small pinch of cinnamon if you would like. For older children and adults, try finely ground walnuts or pumpkin seeds, or a little nut or seed butter. For babies, always make sure nuts and seeds are finely ground or incorporated into a smooth butter to reduce choking risk.
  4. 4 Serve. Mix everything together, or leave the fruit on top for a little texture. Serve immediately.
Honey and babies under 12 months: Honey must not be given to babies under 12 months under any circumstances, due to the risk of infant botulism. Skip the honey entirely for younger babies. Nuts and seeds must always be finely ground for babies and young toddlers, never offered as whole pieces.

Favorite Combinations

🍌 Banana + Walnut

Mashed banana, cottage cheese, and finely ground walnuts make a naturally sweet and creamy combination.

🍐 Pear + Cinnamon

Ripe pear with a tiny pinch of cinnamon is mild, sweet, and especially nice with cottage cheese.

🍑 Peach + Pumpkin Seed

Ripe peach with finely ground pumpkin seeds adds a lovely nutty flavor.

🍓 Just Strawberry

Fresh, ripe strawberries provide enough sweetness on their own, no extras needed. A good one for introducing the bowl for the first time.

Adaptations by Age

AgeHow to serve
6 months+ Choose pasteurized, full-fat cottage cheese. Mash if needed. Use very soft, ripe fruit and mash thoroughly. Walnuts and pumpkin seeds must be finely ground and mixed in rather than offered as pieces. Smooth nut or seed butter also works well mixed in. Do not add honey before 12 months.
Toddlers Can enjoy the bowl with more texture. Try cottage cheese with chopped strawberries and ground pumpkin seeds, or cottage cheese with banana, ground walnuts, and a tiny pinch of cinnamon. Toddlers can help add the fruit and sprinkle the ground nuts or seeds on top themselves.
Adults Make the same bowl with fresh berries, banana, ground walnuts, pumpkin seeds, cinnamon, and a drizzle of raw honey. Add a spoonful of nut butter for a more substantial snack.

Storage

Best fresh

This snack is meant to be assembled fresh each time. It takes only 5 minutes, so keep full-fat cottage cheese and frozen berries on hand and you can put together a fresh bowl anytime.

Easy Mom Shortcut

Keep full-fat cottage cheese and frozen berries in the fridge and freezer. When your child needs a quick snack, scoop some cottage cheese into a bowl, add thawed berries or a banana, and you are done. No cooking, no blender, no complicated ingredients. That is the whole point of this recipe.
