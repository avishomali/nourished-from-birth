A different way to feed your baby

Rooted in ancestral wisdom, backed by research, and shaped by Iranian culinary tradition. Here is what we believe — and why.

🦴

Nourishing Broths & Stocks

The foundation of ancestral infant nutrition. Meat stock and bone broth heal the gut lining, support mineral absorption, and form the base of countless traditional baby foods around the world.

🧠

Real Fats for Brain Development

The low-fat era did real damage. A baby's brain is 60% fat and needs a steady supply of animal fats, egg yolks, and quality oils to develop properly in the first two years of life.

🫙

Fermented & Living Foods

Traditional cultures fermented grains, dairy, and vegetables not as a wellness trend but as necessity. These foods seed the microbiome and build the immune foundation babies need from the start.

🌾

Legumes & Grains Done Right

Soaked, sprouted, or fermented — the way grains and legumes have always been prepared in traditional cultures. This reduces phytic acid, improves digestibility, and makes nutrients available.

🥩

Traditional Whole Foods

Nose-to-tail eating is ancestral, not trendy. Organ meats, marrow, and slow-cooked cuts provide nutrients that muscle meat alone cannot deliver — and babies thrive on them.

🔥

Traditional Preparation Methods

How food is prepared matters as much as what you eat. Slow cooking, soaking, fermenting, and using the whole animal are practices that have nourished healthy populations for thousands of years.

💚

Gut Health from Day One

The gut microbiome is established in the first years of life and shapes immunity, mental health, and metabolic function for decades. What babies eat in this window matters enormously.

🌍

Iranian Heritage

A rich food culture that naturally combines animal foods, legumes, herbs, and grains — ancestral wisdom from birth.

⚖️

Realistic Moderation

Real life exists. Travel, busy weeks, imperfect days. The goal is the foundation — not perfection.
