Meat & Fish

Where We Source Our Protein

Quality matters when you are feeding babies and young children. Wild-caught fish, grass-fed meat, and pasture-raised poultry deliver meaningfully different nutritional profiles than conventional options.

Wild-Caught Fish & Seafood
We use this

Vital Choice Wild Seafood

The wild-caught fish and seafood delivery service we personally use. Flash-frozen within hours of harvest to preserve freshness and nutrition. Sustainably sourced from well-managed fisheries — most are MSC certified or State of Alaska certified. Excellent selection of salmon, sardines, cod, and more. The salmon and sardines are what we use most often in our kitchen for recipes like sardine pate.

Wild-caught fish is consistently lower in contaminants and higher in omega-3s than farmed fish. Vital Choice is one of the most trusted sources in the real food community. We have used them personally and recommend them with confidence.

Cookware

Non-Toxic Pots and Pans

Every cookware material has trade-offs. No option is perfectly inert. We have done the full research — read our honest cookware guide before buying. The products below are what we personally own and use.

Glass · Stovetop
We own this

Visions by Corning Glass-Ceramic Pot

The safest stovetop cooking option we have found. Fully inert glass-ceramic that goes directly on the stovetop — no coatings, no leaching, no concerns. This is what we use for all Persian stews and slow cooking. The broth from a glass pot is noticeably cleaner in taste.

Visions was discontinued by Corning and is only available secondhand. Availability varies. Inspect carefully before buying — check for chips or cracks. A genuinely excellent find if you can locate one in good condition.
Search Secondhand on Amazon
Enameled Cast Iron · Frying
We own this

Le Creuset Signature Cast Iron Everyday Pan

What we use for kotlet, koobideh, and anything that needs good searing and heat retention. Enameled cast iron with no PFAS and no synthetic coating. Excellent heat distribution for Persian pan-fried recipes. It will outlast everything else in the kitchen.

Le Creuset confirmed trace lead on the exterior of some bright-colored pieces. The interior enamel is completely lead-free. For cooking purposes the interior is what matters. A lifetime investment — it does not wear out the way ceramic-coated pans do.
See on Amazon
Enameled Cast Iron · Slow Cooking
We own this

Lodge Enameled Cast Iron Dutch Oven

What we use for large-batch Persian stews when the glass pot is too small. The enamel coating makes it safe for long acidic stews — no nickel leaching like stainless steel, no coating degradation like ceramic pans. The most accessible and affordable enameled cast iron option.

Does not have the same longevity reputation as Le Creuset or Staub but is a solid everyday choice. Inspect the enamel periodically — replace if the interior enamel chips or cracks.

Ceramic-Coated · Everyday
We own this Read the caveats

Our Place 13-Piece Cookware Set

What we use for everyday quick cooking and eggs. PFAS-free, beautiful design, extremely practical 10-in-1 format. We use it at medium heat only, hand-wash every time, and replace it when the coating shows wear.

The "non-toxic" claim is contested. Independent testing found titanium dioxide nanoparticles, siloxanes, lead, and mercury in several ceramic-coated brands including Our Place. The coating also degrades within 1 to 3 years. We use it knowing these trade-offs. Read our full guide before buying.

Salt

Reading That Changed How We Feed Our Family

These are the books that shaped the philosophy behind this site. No affiliate links here — just genuine recommendations.

Nourishing Traditions

Sally Fallon

The foundational text of traditional food preparation. Everything from soaking grains to rendering tallow to making bone broth. Opinionated, sometimes controversial, always worth reading.

Real Food for Pregnancy

The most evidence-based book on nutrition during pregnancy. Grounded in research, practical, and the closest thing to an ancestral nutrition approach backed by mainstream science.

Traditional recipes for babies, toddlers, and beyond. A practical companion to the philosophy of this site — real food from the start, prepared with care.
