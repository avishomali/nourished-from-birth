Our Story

A full-time engineer, a mom of two, and a shelf full of baby food jars that didn't feel right

My name is Avishan. I'm Iranian-born, now raising my family in the US. I have a 2-year-old and a 6-month-old. Until my daughter was a little over a year old I was working full time as a materials engineer — and I still managed to feed her real food most of the time.

Not because I had extra hours in the day. Because once you know a few simple things, it genuinely doesn't take that long. That's what this site is — real food, made practical, for busy moms who already know it matters.
