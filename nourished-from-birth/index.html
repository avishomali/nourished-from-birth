About This Site

Real food, made practical

Real, traditional recipes for babies, toddlers, and the whole family, including the Persian family foods I grew up with, made simple enough for busy, working parents. I'm Avishan, Iranian-born, a mom of two, and a full-time engineer figuring this out one meal at a time.

Real food doesn't have to be perfect.

If you serve one nourishing meal today, that matters. The goal is simply to make real, nourishing food the default as often as you reasonably can.

Our Story

A full-time engineer, a mom of two, and a shelf full of baby food jars that didn't feel right

I'm Avishan, Iranian-born, and now raising my family in the US. Growing up, food was simple and homemade, with no separate "baby food" category. When I became a mother, I found myself reading labels closely and realized how much processed food and hidden sugar had crept into what gets marketed to babies here, so I started making the foods I grew up with instead, simplified for a busy, working parent.
