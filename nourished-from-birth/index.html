Our Story

A full-time engineer, a mom of two, and a shelf full of baby food jars that didn't feel right

My name is Avishan. I'm Iranian-born, now raising my family in the US. I worked full time as a materials engineer until my first daughter turned one — then found out I was pregnant and stepped away. Now my second is 9 months old and I'm heading back to work. My goal is the same it has always been: keep nourishing my kids with real, wholesome food no matter how busy life gets.

There are many wonderful websites teaching authentic Persian recipes. This is not that. My goal here is to keep things simple and practical — fewer ingredients, less complexity, more doable for a busy mom on a regular Tuesday. That's exactly how many Persian grandmothers actually cook: not with a long list of spices, but by letting a few quality ingredients shine. Someone should be able to glance at an ingredient list here and think, "I already have almost everything I need."

Persian food is deeply nourishing by nature. You don't need to make it complicated to keep that nourishment alive. That's what this site is — real food, made practical, for busy moms who already know it matters.
